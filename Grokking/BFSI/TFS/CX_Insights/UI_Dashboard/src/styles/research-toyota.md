# Toyota Financial Services Color Research

## Source Analysis
Colors extracted from toyotafinancial.com and tfs_storyboard_v4.html.

## Primary Brand Colors
- **TFS Red**: `#EB0A1E` — Primary brand color, used for logo, CTAs, key highlights
- **TFS Dark Red**: `#C00000` — Hover/pressed state of red
- **TFS Black**: `#000000` — Text, headers on dark sections
- **TFS Dark Gray**: `#1A1A1A` — Secondary dark, sidebar in original

## Neutral Colors (from website)
- **White**: `#FFFFFF` — Primary background
- **Off-White**: `#F8FAFC` — Page background (light mode)
- **Light Gray**: `#E5E5E5` — Borders, dividers, secondary backgrounds
- **Medium Gray**: `#4A4A4A` — Secondary text, descriptions
- **Dark Gray**: `#1A1A1A` — Primary text on light backgrounds

## Semantic Colors (from storyboard)
- **Success Green**: `#28A745` — Positive sentiment, good status
- **Warning Amber**: `#FFC107` — Warnings, medium severity
- **Error Red**: `#DC3545` — Errors, negative sentiment (distinct from brand red)
- **Info Blue**: `#0047AB` — Informational, links, primary paths

## Usage Guidelines (per PROJECT_GUIDE.md)
- **Dark colors (black/red) used SPARINGLY** — accents and highlights only
- **NOT for entire widgets or large areas**
- Red should be: button accents, trend indicators, alert highlights, active states
- Black should be: text only, not backgrounds (except possibly nav bar)
- **Predominantly light/neutral palette** for the 90% Apple aesthetic

## Color Application Rules
1. **Page background**: Off-white (#F8FAFC)
2. **Widget/card background**: White (#FFFFFF)
3. **Primary text**: Near-black (#0F172A for softer look than pure black)
4. **Secondary text**: Medium gray (#64748B)
5. **Borders**: Light gray (#E2E8F0)
6. **Accent (primary CTA)**: TFS Red (#EB0A1E) — small touches only
7. **Accent hover**: TFS Dark Red (#C00000)
8. **Success**: Green (#22C55E or #28A745)
9. **Warning**: Amber (#F59E0B or #FFC107)
10. **Error**: Red (#EF4444 or #DC3545) — NOT brand red
11. **Info**: Blue (#3B82F6 or #0047AB)
