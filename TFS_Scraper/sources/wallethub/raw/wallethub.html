1 Star Rating
I am very dissatisfied with my experience with Toyota Financial Services. I completed my 72-month contract, yet I was told I still owe additional money. Despite explaining my financial hardship, Toyota Financial Services refused to negotiate, offer any flexibility, or work toward a reasonable solution. Instead, the company’s only focus has been on collecting the full balance, with no willingness to consider my situation.

To make matters worse, many of the representatives who contacted me were rude, unprofessional, and dismissive. Toyota should hire more qualified and professional staff who can listen and help customers, instead of making the process even more stressful.

The only positive part of my experience has been with the Resolution Specialists, who at least took the time to explain things calmly and respectfully. Unfortunately, the overall experience has been so negative that I would not recommend financing a vehicle through Toyota to anyone. This was my first car purchase, and Toyota Financial has left me feeling taken advantage of.

I am requesting a full review of my account and a fair resolution.
